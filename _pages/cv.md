Research Interests
======
Visual understanding and reasoning, vision-language models, text-video retrieval, multimodal agents, computational imaging, and image super-resolution. My goal is to develop intelligent systems that can explore, address, and exploit visual uncertainty for improved performance, trustworthiness, and reduced hallucination.
