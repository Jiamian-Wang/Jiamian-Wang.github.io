My research aims to build intelligent systems that can **explore**, **address**, and **exploit** visual uncertainty — for better performance, trustworthiness, and reduced hallucination. My latest interest centers on three interconnected directions, the **3 A's**.

## Agent
How can we build scalable and controllable *environments* that teach an agent to perceive, reason, and act across multi-turn, multimodal interactions — and how should we *train* such agents?

- Turning raw, unstructured data (e.g., documents) into controllable training environments for search/QA agents.
- Training agents that decide *when* and *how* to call multimodal tools across multiple turns.

*Representative work:* DocArena (2025); the document search agent developed at Adobe Research.

## Algorithm
What post-training *algorithms* — including Bayesian-grounded ones — can push the rich signals from an environment into the agent more efficiently and losslessly?

- Preference optimization and self-refinement for autoregressive / visual models.
- Uncertainty-aware modeling and alignment to close the vision–language semantics gap.

*Representative work:* Visual Self-Refinement (EMNLP 2025); Text is MASS (CVPR 2024 Highlight); Modeling Mask Uncertainty (ECCV 2022 Oral).

## Autonomy
Can we develop autonomous agents that one day help human drivers make safer, real-time decisions?

- Command-aligned guidance and post-training for VLA-based autonomous-driving models.
- Bridging multimodal reasoning with real-world, safety-critical decision making.

*Representative work:* Latent-Centroid Steering (IROS 2026); VLA model post-training at NVIDIA.
